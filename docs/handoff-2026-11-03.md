### 3. A personal style, for creators

Brand kits suit companies. People with a personal brand who post reels need a two-minute setup. Put it beside "New kit" in Brand kits, in two ways: **Personal style** (new) and **Brand kit from a guideline** (the existing `KitMaker`).

- **Name or handle**, and an optional photo or mark, which goes in `logos`.
- **Three or four colours:** ground, text, accent, and an optional second accent. Use the studio's DialKit colour rows (`color-row.tsx`), never a native colour picker.
- **Fonts:** a heading and a text face, picked from a short list of OFL fonts shipped with the studio, or uploaded.
- **A few rules:**
  - energy (Calm | Punchy)
  - captions (On | Off, and their style)
  - words (Casual | Formal)
  - one free-text line, for things like "lowercase captions, no emojis"
- **Save it as an ordinary `brand.json` kit**, so renders and Claude's context need no change. Fill the `ramps` and `texture` from the picked colours the way the kit maker does.
